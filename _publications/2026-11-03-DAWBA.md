## Summary

This preregistered study investigates whether free-text responses from adolescents and parents capture information about the impact of adolescent depression that is not reflected in structured symptom questionnaires. We analyzed responses from 153 adolescents and 106 parents in the Development and Well-Being Assessment (DAWBA), using large language models to extract information corresponding to a taxonomy of 80 psychological constructs and a sequential modeling framework to evaluate the incremental predictive information of text.

Parent narratives contained information about reported depression impact beyond that available in structured symptom responses, particularly in domains related to everyday functioning. We did not find corresponding evidence of additional predictive information in adolescent self-reports. Given the moderate sample size, the results are preliminary; the study primarily demonstrates an approach for evaluating the unique information provided by free-text clinical and psychological data.
